The Deep Work Principle

“High productivity in university is never measured by the hours you surrender to fatigue, but by the clarity and retention you extract from uninterrupted attention.”

— The Student Corner Editorial Board
Strategy: The 90-Minute Focus Protocol View Weekly Schedule Guide →
01

The Two-Minute Rule

If an administrative collegiate task takes less than two minutes—such as submitting an attendance form or emailing a tutor—complete it immediately to prevent mental clutter.

02

Single-Tasking Deep Blocks

Switching between seminar readings and social feeds breaks your concentration. Choose a manageable focus block and keep your phone out of reach.

03

Spaced Retrieval Practice

Review new lecture material within 24 hours for ten minutes. One hour of revision across four separate days beats four hours crammed into a panicked night.

04

Circadian Sleep Protection

Your brain consolidates memory traces into long-term neocortex storage during deep sleep. Sacrificing sleep directly undermines the study hours you invested.

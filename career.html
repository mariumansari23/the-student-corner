Four-Year Roadmap

The Student Career Development Timeline

What ambitious undergraduates should focus on during each year of their degree.

🌱

Year 1: Foundation & Curiosity

Focus on achieving a strong academic foundation (GPA). Join one career-focused student society, attend campus employer talks, and explore industry sectors to identify what sparks your curiosity.

💻

Year 2: Skills & Projects

Build demonstrable artifacts outside the classroom. Complete personal projects, build a clean portfolio website, learn version control (Git), and apply for summer insight weeks or sophomore internships.

💼

Year 3: Summer Internships

The marquee recruiting year. Submit polished applications early in the autumn semester (August–October). Practice STAR behavioural interview answers and complete daily technical problem-solving.

🎓

Year 4: Graduate Conversion

Convert summer internships into return full-time offers, or submit targeted applications to graduate rotational programs. Finalize your capstone thesis and negotiate early career compensation.

🤝

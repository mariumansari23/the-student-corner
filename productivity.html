Mental Models

Six Productivity Frameworks for University

Field-tested frameworks that eliminate decision fatigue and establish productive momentum.

🍅

1. The 50/10 Pomodoro Protocol

Standard 25-minute sprints can feel too brief for intensive problem sets. Try 50 minutes of deep immersion followed by a strict 10-minute walkaway break with no screens.

📊

2. The Eisenhower Matrix

Sort tasks into Urgent vs. Important. Real academic progress happens in Quadrant 2 (Important, Not Urgent)—such as starting essays weeks before the deadline.

⏳

3. The 5-Minute Rule

When dreading an assignment, commit to working on it for just five minutes. If you still want to quit after five minutes, you can. 85% of the time, the initial friction dissolves.

🚫

4. Digital Distraction Quarantine

Keep your smartphone in another room while studying. Research demonstrates that the mere physical presence of a phone on your desk drains cognitive processing bandwidth.

🗓️

5. Sunday Weekly Planning

Dedicate 30 minutes every Sunday evening to review upcoming deadlines, map lecture hours, and block out revision times. You enter Monday with total clarity instead of dread.

🎯

6. The Daily Big Three

Never write a to-do list with 25 items. Every morning, choose exactly three core academic tasks that must get done today. Everything else is secondary bonus work.

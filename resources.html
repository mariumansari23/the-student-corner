02. Information Synthesis

Comparison of Note-Taking Methods

Different subjects demand different note architectures. Find the right model for your modules.

📋

The Cornell Method

Best for: Lecture-heavy courses and humanities.

Divides the page into a 2.5-inch cue column, a 6-inch note area, and a 2-inch summary section at the bottom for instant self-quizzing.

Read Cornell Tutorial →
🌳

Mind Mapping & Concept Trees

Best for: Systems biology, anatomy, and conceptual theory.

Connects central ideas outward in hierarchical branches, reinforcing relational understanding and visual memory.

Explore Visual Synthesis →
📦

The Boxing Method

Best for: Modular topics and comparative studies.

Encapsulates related formulas, sub-topics, or historical periods into discrete geometrical boxes for rapid visual chunking.

Learn Boxing Technique →
